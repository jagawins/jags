The Research Computing Readiness Model
Five layers that must be built coherently and in sequence. Most health systems have Layer 1. Few have all five. The gap between Layer 1 and Layer 5 is where pharmaceutical partnerships are declined, federal grants go to better-equipped competitors, and research faculty recruits choose elsewhere.

Research Area 01
Multimodal AI: Where the Next Decade of Clinical Research Lives
The most powerful clinical AI models will not be trained on a single data type. Genomic sequence, medical imaging, continuous sensor streams, and structured EHR data each capture a different dimension of patient biology. Integrating them into a unified research compute architecture is the defining infrastructure challenge of the next decade.

Research Area 02
HPC Architecture for Academic Medical Centers
Modern research HPC is a layered architecture — not a monolithic cluster. Workload management governs access across compute tiers. Storage architecture determines whether the GPU investment delivers throughput or sits idle. Cloud burst handles peak demand without displacing concurrent research jobs. Getting each layer right requires different expertise.

HPC architecture stack — from researcher job submission through workload governance, compute tiers, parallel storage, and cloud burst overflow

Research Area 03
From Patient Care to Scientific Evidence: The 8-Stage Pipeline
Clinical data does not become research evidence automatically. It flows through a series of transformation, governance, and compute stages — each with its own quality bar, compliance requirement, and infrastructure dependency. Most health systems have stages 1 and 2 functioning. Research-ready institutions have governed, automated pathways through all eight.

The research informatics pipeline — from patient care through governance, HPC compute, and model validation to scientific and commercial impact

Research Area 04
Federated Research Computing: Cross-Institutional Science Without Moving Data
The most important research questions require patient populations no single institution has. Federated computing enables multi-institutional research without PHI ever leaving the originating institution — through federated query, privacy-preserving record linkage, and federated model training with NVIDIA FLARE.

Federated research computing network — multi-institutional collaboration using aggregate query results and model weights, never raw PHI

Research Area 05
GPU Economics: The Build vs. Burst Decision Framework
The on-premises versus cloud GPU decision is an economics question with a calculable break-even point. Based on analysis across multiple institutional contexts, that threshold falls between 25,000 and 35,000 annual GPU-hours. Below it, cloud burst is economically rational. Above it, on-premises hardware generates substantial savings while providing dedicated availability for continuous research pipelines.

GPU economics decision framework — on-premises vs. cloud burst break-even at approximately 35,000 annual GPU-hours for an 8-node H100 cluster configuration

Vision
Where This Is Going: Research Ambitions for 2026–2040
The health systems that will lead biomedical research in the next fifteen years are not necessarily those with the largest endowments. They are the ones that invest deliberately in the infrastructure layers that let them say yes — to the grant, to the trial, to the partnership, to the science.
NEAR TERM — 1 TO 3 YEARS
GPU-accelerated genomics becomes standard clinical infrastructure, not research specialty. AI-enhanced trial recruitment powered by i2b2 + NLP cuts enrollment timelines by 50–60% at AMCs that implement it. HIPAA-compliant cloud burst replaces custom HPC for 60% of batch research workloads.
Key milestones: NVIDIA Clara Parabricks adoption · Cloud BAA governance maturity · i2b2 AI layer deployment
MID TERM — 3 TO 7 YEARS
Federated learning becomes the default model for multi-site clinical research. Regional HPC networks — health systems anchoring shared compute for affiliated academic institutions — replace siloed institutional clusters. The first clinical foundation models trained on federated multimodal data across 10+ institutions go into production.
Key milestones: FLARE production deployments · Regional HPC consortia · OMOP federated networks at scale
LONG TERM — 7 TO 15 YEARS
Real-time clinical-research integration: the boundary between care delivery and research participation disappears. Every patient encounter contributes to population-scale research with appropriate consent and governance. Pharmaceutical development cycles compress from 12–15 years to 7–9 years through AI-enabled target identification, adaptive trial design, and AI-enhanced recruitment.
Key milestones: Continuous consent platforms · Adaptive trial infrastructure · Real-world evidence at clinical scale
